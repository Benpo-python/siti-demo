About

Two joiners, one workshop, no shortcuts.

Tom and Ellie started Harbour & Oak in 2016 after a decade in boat-building and furniture restoration. Everything we make is cut, joined and finished by hand in our workshop at the Paintworks.

We use FSC-certified timber, give you a fixed written quote and fit every piece ourselves, so the people who measure your home are the people who build for it.

FAQ

FAQ

Is the home survey really free?

Yes. We visit, measure and talk through ideas at no cost and with no obligation, anywhere within 25 miles of Bristol.

How long does a project take?

Most wardrobes and alcove units are made and fitted within 4 to 6 weeks of sign-off. Kitchens and staircases take 8 to 12 weeks.

Do you give fixed prices?

Yes. After the survey you get a fixed written quote. The price only changes if you change the design.

What timber do you use?

FSC-certified oak, ash and walnut, plus high-quality birch ply and MDF for painted finishes.

Do you offer a guarantee?

Every piece comes with a 10-year guarantee on joinery and fitting.
