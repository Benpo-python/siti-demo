FAQ

FAQ

Do I need an appointment?

Yes, we work by appointment only so we can give you proper time. Book by phone, WhatsApp or using the form.

What is plant-based colour?

A blend of dye plants (henna, indigo, cassia) that colours without ammonia and coats the hair fibre.

How long does a balayage take?

Allow 2.5 to 3.5 hours depending on length and the look you want.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Card, cash and contactless. Gift cards are available at the salon.

How do I get to the salon?

Metro A, Cordeliers station, a 3-minute walk. Saint-Antoine car park right next door.
